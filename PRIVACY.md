# GravityNote privacy policy

GravityNote stores your documents and preferences locally on your Mac. The app does not collect or transmit your notes, maps, images, usage data, or personal information to the developer. It includes no analytics, advertising, accounts, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.

Files and folders you choose through macOS file dialogs are accessed only to provide the document features you request. If you place a document in a folder managed by a cloud storage provider, that provider handles synchronization under its own privacy policy.

Apple handles the purchase and may provide its standard App Store reports under Apple's privacy policy.

For support or privacy questions, [open an issue](https://github.com/1234igor/gravitynote/issues). Please do not include private document contents.

Last updated: 21 September 2026.
